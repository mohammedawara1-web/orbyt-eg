04 Process

Brief to delivery — in days, not weeks.

A tight three-step loop. A single product shoot runs Day 0 to Day 2. Bigger campaigns keep the same rhythm on a longer timeline.

/ 01 · Direct · Day 0

We listen & frame.

A 10-minute WhatsApp brief, then we lock the mood, references, and shot list into one clear visual direction before a single frame is made.

  • You send: product info, references, brand assets
  • You get: a shot list and visual direction
/ 02 · Render · Day 1

We render & cut.

Our director drives the best generative tools and reviews every frame against the brief. Nothing ships on autopilot.

  • Cinematic lighting, brand-locked palette
  • A first look sent for your notes
/ 03 · Deliver · Day 2

We ship & refine.

Every aspect ratio and format, delivered ready to post. Then we refine over revision rounds until it is pixel-right.

  • All formats: 1:1 · 4:5 · 9:16 · 16:9
  • Revisions included until you approve

08 FAQ

Questions, answered.

If yours is not here, message us on WhatsApp. We usually reply within minutes.

Is this real photography or AI?

Both worlds, the best of each. We create with the leading AI tools, but a human director frames, lights, and reviews every shot. You get studio-grade polish without the studio logistics.

How fast do I get my shots?

A single product shoot is delivered in 48 hours. Larger campaigns are scoped to your launch date and usually land in a few days, not weeks.

How many revisions do I get?

We refine until the frames are right for your brand. Revision rounds are included in every package.

What formats and sizes do you deliver?

Every aspect ratio your channels need: 1:1 and 4:5 for feed, 9:16 for reels and stories, 16:9 for landing pages and YouTube. High-resolution exports, ready to post.

Do I own the images? Can I run them in paid ads?

Yes. You receive full commercial usage rights for the visuals we deliver, cleared for organic and paid campaigns.

What do you need from me to start?

Your product details, any brand assets or references, and a sentence on the vibe you want. That is enough for us to build the shot list and get moving.

How do payments work?

We confirm the quote on WhatsApp, take a deposit to start, and settle the balance on delivery.
